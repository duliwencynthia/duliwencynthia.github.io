About

I'm a PhD student in Computer Science at North Carolina State University, where I also earned my Master of Computer Science. My current work looks at how LLM-based agents can communicate like good teammates: negotiating plans, coordinating actions, and being polite in a way that fits the context rather than as decoration.

Before that I worked across NLP, machine learning for cybersecurity, empirical software engineering, and full-stack development.

Research

Human–AI InteractionJul 2025 – Aug 2026

Beyond Being Nice, Cook Up Collaboration: Context-Aware Politeness for Human–LLM Teams

  • Developed Plato, an LLM-based collaborative agent that negotiates plans, coordinates actions, and carries out shared tasks with humans in Overcooked.
  • Designed a context-aware politeness framework grounded in Brown–Levinson and Spencer–Oatey. It turns social face, autonomy, and interactional goals into concrete communication strategies.
  • Built an end-to-end human–LLM interaction system: structured prompting, dialogue management, conversation summarization, game-state feedback, and LLM-generated actions for real-time collaboration.
  • Led a human-subject study from start to finish: experimental protocol, counterbalancing, recruitment, sessions, and behavioral and survey analysis. Plato was compared against a human Wizard-of-Oz and a non-politeness AI baseline.
