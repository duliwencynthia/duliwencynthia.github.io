/01 — about-me

A researcher who listens.

I'm Liwen Du, but please call me Cynthia. I'm a PhD student in Computer Science at North Carolina State University, where I also earned my Master of Computer Science. My research asks how LLM-based agents can communicate like good collaborators: negotiating plans, coordinating actions, and being polite in a way that fits the context rather than as decoration.

Along the way I've worked on NLP for medical dialogue, machine learning for cyber-threat intelligence, empirical software engineering, and full-stack web development.

/02 — research

Teaching AI to be a good teammate.

featuredjul 2025 — aug 2026

Beyond Being Nice, Cook Up Collaboration: Context-Aware Politeness for Human–LLM Teams

  • Developed Plato, an LLM-based collaborative agent that negotiates plans, coordinates actions, and carries out shared tasks with humans in Overcooked.
  • Designed a context-aware politeness framework grounded in Brown–Levinson and Spencer–Oatey. It turns social face, autonomy, and interactional goals into concrete communication strategies.
  • Built an end-to-end human–LLM interaction system: structured prompting, dialogue management, conversation summarization, game-state feedback, and LLM-generated actions for real-time collaboration.
  • Led a human-subject study from start to finish: protocol, counterbalancing, recruitment, sessions, and analysis. Plato was compared against a human Wizard-of-Oz and a non-politeness AI baseline.

ml · securityfeb — may 2025

Advancing Cyberthreat Analysis with MITRE ATT&CK Reports

An ML pipeline that classifies cyber threats from MITRE ATT&CK-aligned intelligence reports. It uses SMOTE for class imbalance and Random Projection for efficiency. The fine-tuned RoBERTa model cut training time by 35% and made minority-class results more stable.

Could Refactoring Help with Software Migration?

A study of how code smells and refactoring patterns affect large-scale software migration. I tested the hypotheses through structured interviews with professional engineers.
